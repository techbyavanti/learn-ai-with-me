# Your data

You own your data. This page explains how you can export it, how long we keep it, and
where we store it.

## Export your data

You can export your data as CSV or JSON from the Account page. The export includes all
projects, tasks, comments, and the names of attached files. Attached files are not in the
export. Download them from each task, or use the API to download them in bulk.

Large exports can take some minutes. We send you an email with a download link when the
export is ready. The link is valid for 48 hours.

## Deleted projects

When you delete a project, it goes to the Trash. We keep deleted projects for 30 days.
After 30 days, we remove them, and nobody can bring them back. To restore a project,
open the Trash and select 'Restore'. Only admins and the owner of the project can restore it.

## Backups

We make a backup of all workspaces every 6 hours. We keep each backup for 35 days. Backups
are for disaster recovery only. We cannot restore one project or one task from a backup
for you. Use the Trash for that.

## Where we store your data

We store your data in data centers in the European Union by default. Workspaces on the
paid plan can select a data center in the United States when they create the workspace.
You cannot move a workspace to another region later.

All data is encrypted in transit and at rest.

## Delete your account

To delete your account, open the Account page and select 'Delete account'. If you are the
last admin of a workspace, you must first make another person an admin, or delete the
workspace. We remove your personal data within 30 days.
