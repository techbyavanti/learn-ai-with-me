# Contact and support

There are many ways to get help. Most questions have an answer in this help guide, so
search here first.

## Email support

Support replies to emails in 24 hours on business days. Customers on the paid plan get
priority support, and we reply to them in 4 hours on business days. Write in English,
German, or Spanish. Please include your workspace name and, if possible, a screenshot.

## Live chat

Live chat is available on the paid plan from 9:00 to 17:00 Central European Time, Monday
to Friday. Open the chat from the help menu in the app.

## Our office

Our office opens at 8:00 and closes at 18:00, Monday to Friday. The office is closed on
public holidays. You can visit us without an appointment, but for a product demo, please
book a time on our website. There is a public parking garage next to the building.

## Status page

When there is a problem with our service, we post an update on our status page in
15 minutes. You can subscribe to the status page to get updates by email.
