# Plans and billing

This page explains our plans, how we charge you, and what to do when something goes
wrong with a payment. All prices are in US dollars and do not include local taxes.

## Plans

We have two plans. The free plan is for small teams and personal use. It includes
3 projects and up to 5 users. The paid plan has no limit on projects or users. It also
adds the timeline view, guest access, and priority support.

You can change your plan at any time on the Billing page. When you upgrade, the new
features are available immediately. When you downgrade, your projects stay, but you can
only edit 3 of them. You select which 3 projects stay active.

## Prices

The paid plan costs 12 dollars for each user, each month. If you pay for a full year at
one time, the price is 10 dollars for each user, each month. Teams with more than 50 users
can ask our sales team for a custom price. Nonprofit organizations and schools get a
discount of 50 percent. To ask for this discount, send us a document that shows your
status.

We charge you for the number of users on the first day of each billing period. If you add
users during the period, we charge a part of the price for the remaining days.

## Invoices and payment methods

We send an invoice by email after each payment. You can also download all your invoices
as PDF files from the Billing page. We accept credit cards and debit cards. Customers on
the yearly plan can also pay by bank transfer. Bank transfers must arrive within 30 days
of the invoice date.

If a card payment fails, we try again after 3 days and after 7 days. We send you an email
each time. If the third try fails, your workspace changes to the free plan. You do not lose
data, and you can upgrade again at any time.

## Refunds

You can ask for a refund in the first 14 days after a payment. Send the request from the
Billing page, or write to our support team. We check each request and reply in 2 business
days. After we approve the request, the money goes back to your card. This takes 5 to 7
business days. Your bank can need more time to show the money in your account.

We do not give refunds for the unused part of a month after the first 14 days. Instead,
you can downgrade, and the change starts at the next billing period.
