# Mobile app

The mobile app lets you see and update your tasks when you are not at your desk.

## Download the app

The mobile app is available for iOS and Android. You need iOS 16 or later, or Android 11
or later. Sign in with the same account that you use on the web. If your workspace uses
single sign-on, the app opens your company sign-in page.

## Work offline

The app saves your recent projects on your phone. You can read and edit tasks without an
internet connection. When the connection comes back, the app sends your changes. If
another person changed the same task at the same time, the app keeps both versions and
asks you which version to keep.

## Notifications

You get a push notification when someone assigns a task to you, mentions you in a comment,
or changes the due date of your task. To change which notifications you get, open the app
settings and select Notifications. You can also set quiet hours, for example from 20:00 to
7:00, when the app does not send notifications.

## Widgets

On iOS and Android, you can add a widget to your home screen. The widget shows the tasks
that are due today.
