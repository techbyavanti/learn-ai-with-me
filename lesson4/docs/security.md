# Account and security

We take the security of your data seriously. This page explains how you can protect your
account, and what we do to protect your workspace.

## Passwords

Your password must have at least 12 characters. We check new passwords against a list of
passwords that were leaked in data breaches, and we do not accept those passwords.
We never send your password by email, and our support team cannot see it.

To reset your password, open Settings and select 'Forgot password'. We send you a link by
email. The link is valid for one hour. If the link expires, ask for a new one.

## Two-factor authentication

Two-factor authentication is available for all plans. When you turn it on, you must enter
a code from an authenticator app each time you sign in on a new device. Save your backup
codes in a safe place. Each backup code works only one time.

Admins on the paid plan can make two-factor authentication required for every user in the
workspace. Users who do not turn it on cannot open the workspace until they do.

## Single sign-on

Workspaces on the paid plan with more than 20 users can use single sign-on (SSO) with
SAML. Supported identity providers include Okta, Microsoft Entra ID, and Google Workspace.
When SSO is on, users sign in with their company account, and password sign-in is turned
off for them.

## Sessions and devices

You can see all the devices that are signed in to your account on the Security page.
Select a device and click 'Sign out' to end that session. A session ends automatically
after 30 days without activity. When you change your password, we sign you out on all
other devices.

## Report a security problem

If you find a security problem, write to our security team. Do not share the details in
public. We reply to security reports in one business day.
